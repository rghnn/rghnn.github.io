Bio

I am a PhD student at MIT Sloan. My current research focuses on Quantitative Marketing, Economics of Data and Competition, Industrial Organization, and AI.

Before my PhD, I was a researcher at the Rotman School of Management and the TD Management Data and Analytics Lab at the University of Toronto, working with Avi Goldfarb and Ryan Webb. In another pre-econ life, I spent time at Cohere as a machine learning researcher, where I worked on large representational language models. Earlier research projects include generative models, NLP, MCMC and sampling methods, reinforcement learning, and next-generation wireless communications.
